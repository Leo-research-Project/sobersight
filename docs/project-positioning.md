# SoberSight — Project Positioning

## Target

SoberSight targets a **self-administered, few-second readiness check for safety-critical operators**, especially remote or console-based operators who may not have an on-site trained administrator or dedicated alcohol-testing hardware.

The intended use is a pre-shift or periodic **fitness-for-duty / readiness screen**, not an evidential BAC/BrAC measurement.

## Positioning

SoberSight is positioned as an **active ocular readiness check based on involuntary physiological biomarkers**:

- pupillary light reflex (PLR);
- horizontal gaze nystagmus;
- vertical gaze nystagmus.

The core design premise is that these ocular responses are less voluntarily gameable than reaction-time, typing, gait, or attention tasks when the user is motivated to pass.

## Clinical / operational boundary

SoberSight should be framed as a **screening/readiness-support system**. Evidential breath or blood testing remains the legal standard where BAC/BrAC proof is required.

## Validation direction

The source deck proposes controlled alcohol dosing with:
- BrAC ground truth;
- Standardized Field Sobriety Test (SFST);
- Biphasic Alcohol Effects Scale (B-BAES).
